# Frequently asked questions

## Is the SetFlow source code public?

No. This repository is a source-free product case study. It contains reviewed
screenshots, conceptual diagrams, decisions, evidence boundaries, and
limitations—not application source or proprietary architecture.

## Is SetFlow available in the App Store?

No availability claim is made. SetFlow is presented as a private in-development
product, and this package does not establish production deployment or release
readiness.

## Do the screenshots contain personal workout or health data?

The five included images were captured in a dedicated iOS Simulator demo/test
state and reviewed before inclusion. They do not contain a real user's account,
health, or workout data.

## Does the export back up or restore the application?

No. The current case study describes versioned CSV and JSON workout reports
marked `REPORT_ONLY` and `restorable=false`. They are readable reports, not a
database backup, restore point, or synchronization mechanism.

## Is every product area shown at runtime?

No. Home, Workout Hangar, Fuel, Pilot, and Profile are represented in the public
simulator captures. Wellness and Data are supported by private source and route
evidence but are not shown in the current public runtime set.

## Are wellness or fuel features medical advice?

No. The case study does not claim clinical interpretation, medical advice, or
health outcomes.

## Does SetFlow currently ship an AI coach or LLM feature?

No. The product direction explores reviewable contextual guidance, but this case
study does not claim a deployed LLM, retrieval-augmented generation system,
fine-tuned model, or autonomous coach. Those ideas remain future direction until
separate current evidence exists.

## How is AI used in the development workflow?

AI assistance supports problem decomposition, implementation options, debugging,
documentation, and audit work. Suggestions remain subject to human review and
proportional source, build, test, and runtime verification. Generated output is
not treated as evidence by itself.

## What can a reviewer evaluate here?

A reviewer can evaluate product framing, navigation and state reasoning,
persistence and failure-state thinking, report-contract clarity, public-proof
discipline, and the visible design represented in the reviewed screenshots.

## What would require separate proof?

Physical-device behavior, production performance, complete accessibility,
privacy and security signoff, distribution, adoption, revenue, and release
readiness all require separate current evidence.
