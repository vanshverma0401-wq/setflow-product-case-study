# Public feature matrix

## Evidence states

Runtime demonstrated

Source/reachability evidenced

Future direction

Not claimed

These states keep visual proof, private-source verification, roadmap thinking,
and excluded claims separate.

| Public statement | Evidence state | Public wording | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home, Hangar, Fuel, Pilot, and Profile visuals | Runtime demonstrated | Five reviewed simulator captures show these visible surfaces in demo/test state | A capture does not establish production, device, privacy, or release readiness |
| Six primary product areas | Source/reachability evidenced | Routes to Home, Wellness, Fuel, Pilot, Data, and Profile | Do not imply equal completeness across areas |
| Workout planning | Source/reachability evidenced | Home connects to a structured session builder | Do not publish source or internal route details |
| Active-workout experience | Source/reachability evidenced | The private product includes a live-session workflow | Do not claim production runtime quality |
| Workout reports | Source/reachability evidenced | Generates versioned CSV and JSON workout reports | Always state `REPORT_ONLY` and non-restorable |
| Storage recovery states | Source/reachability evidenced | Models visible degraded-storage and recovery states | Do not call this a complete backup/restore system |
| SwiftUI and SwiftData | Source/reachability evidenced | Built as a private SwiftUI/SwiftData iOS product | Do not disclose proprietary architecture |
| AI-assisted engineering workflow | Future direction | Use coding-agent assistance for problem decomposition, implementation options, debugging, documentation, and evidence review under human verification | This is a process disclosure, not a shipped in-product AI feature or proof that generated output is correct |
| Contextual training intelligence | Future direction | Explore reviewable guidance that can connect workout, wellness, fuel, progress, and history context with rationale and user control | Do not imply a deployed LLM, RAG system, fine-tuned model, autonomous coach, medical advice, or a delivery date |
| Broader product evolution | Future direction | Improve the coherence, reliability, and usefulness of the training journey | Do not publish unreleased implementation plans or promise dates |
| App Store release, production deployment, adoption, revenue, or health outcomes | Not claimed | This case study does not establish these outcomes | Add only after separate, current verification |

## Publication rule

If a future source review cannot re-establish a statement, remove or soften it.
Roadmap intent, filenames, or an old screenshot are not sufficient evidence.
