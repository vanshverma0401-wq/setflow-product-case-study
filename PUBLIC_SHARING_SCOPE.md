# Public sharing scope

SetFlow is an iPhone strength-training app in development before beta. This repository is a product case study, not the application source or a downloadable app.

The public package is limited to explanatory documents, dated interface examples, original conceptual diagrams and checks for the case-study files. Native source, credentials, private user records, signing material and internal development artifacts are outside this package.

App examples use fictional or synthetic data and retain their capture dates and limits. They do not establish current app correctness, clinical validation, device acceptance or release readiness. Development used substantial coding-agent assistance; product direction and review are separate from independently implementing every component.

The website is a separate review presentation. Its registration design is simulated and noncollecting; it creates no subscription or beta admission.

Third-party materials retain their applicable rights and attribution. See [asset attribution](ASSET_ATTRIBUTION.md) and [viewing and reuse terms](CASE_STUDY_USAGE.md). A case-study copyright statement does not grant ownership of third-party material or certify all upstream provenance.

Public-package checks are limited to this repository's permitted files and document contracts. They do not certify production security, privacy-law compliance or legal clearance. Do not include credentials or personal records in public issues or contributions.
